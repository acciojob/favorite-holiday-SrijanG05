Kyoto, Japan


Kyoto is the cultural heart of Japan, famous for its classical Buddhist temples, as well as gardens, imperial palaces, Shinto shrines, and traditional wooden houses. It offers a unique glimpse into the country's rich history and serene natural beauty, especially during the cherry blossom and autumn foliage seasons.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Fushimi Inari-taisha: Famous for its thousands of vermilion torii gates that straddle a network of trails.
  2. Kinkaku-ji: Also known as the Golden Pavilion, this Zen temple has its top two floors completely covered in gold leaf.
  3. Arashiyama Bamboo Grove: A path where you can walk through towering stalks of green bamboo that sway in the wind.
  4. Kiyomizu-dera: An iconic wooden temple known for its large wooden stage that offers great views of the city.
  5. Gion District: Kyoto's most famous geisha district, filled with traditional teahouses and boutiques.

Must-Try Foods

  • Kaiseki Ryori: A traditional multi-course dinner that is considered the pinnacle of Japanese haute cuisine.
  • Matcha Sweets: Various treats made from premium green tea powder sourced from nearby Uji.
  • Yudofu: Soft tofu simmered in a light broth, which is a staple of Buddhist temple cuisine.
  • Yatsuhashi: A popular souvenir snack made of glutinous rice flour and cinnamon.
